The frontier is not what it seems

You are the Commander of the FRV Aurora, a Frontier Response Vessel dispatched to investigate incidents on humanity's expanding edge. The United Earth Alliance has built a utopia: 150 years of peace, cooperation, and exploration.

But something is wrong on the frontier. And the more you investigate, the less certain anything becomes.

What's inside

Your crew, your choices

Four specialists, four perspectives. Every decision shapes your relationships with Kira, Marcus, Amara, and Jax, and what they'll trust you with.

X-Files meets Star Trek

Investigate impossible phenomena in humanity's optimistic future. Each case challenges what's supposed to exist at all.

Episodic mystery

Each episode is a complete investigation that builds toward something larger. Play one at a time, or binge the whole season.

Fully voiced

Every character brought to life with full voice acting and an original atmospheric soundtrack.

Multiple endings

Your philosophy matters. The ending you reach reflects not just your choices, but your worldview.
