Support

We read everything. We answer the rest.

How can we help?

The Aurora Chronicles is built by one person at Entropy. There's no support ticket queue and no bot on the other end — just email. Reach out about any of the below and you'll get a real reply.

Bugs & crashes

Something broke, soft-locked, or won't launch? Tell us your platform (Steam, iOS, Android), the episode, and what happened. Screenshots and logs help a lot.

Saves & progress

Your progress is saved locally on your device, and on Steam it also syncs to Steam Cloud. We don't keep copies on our servers, so a deleted or corrupted local save usually can't be restored — but if a save won't load when it should, that's a bug, and we'd like to hear about it.

Your data & privacy

Request a copy or deletion of the data associated with your anonymous player identifier. See the Privacy Policy for what we store and your rights.

Press & business

Press kits, review keys, curator requests, and partnership inquiries are all welcome at the same address below.

Contact

To cut down on spam, the email address is revealed after a quick human check.

Verifying you are human…

We aim to reply within a few days. Including your platform and a clear description speeds things up.
