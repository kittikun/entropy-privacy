Effective date: June 4, 2026

1. What This Page Covers

This notice explains how the entropy.kittikun.jp website handles analytics and cookies. It is separate from the game's Privacy Policy, which covers data collected inside The Aurora Chronicles on Steam, iOS, and Android.

2. Analytics We Use

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit this website and where they come from — for example, total visits, which pages are viewed, the referring site or search, and approximate location (country or region). This helps us see which announcements and channels are reaching people.

We use this only in aggregate. We do not build advertising profiles, we do not show ads, and we do not sell or share this data for advertising. Ad-related and personalization signals are disabled.

3. Cookies

When analytics is active, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (named _ga and _ga_<id>) containing a randomly generated identifier used to distinguish returning from new visits. These cookies do not contain your name, email, or anything you type. They expire automatically (up to about two years) and can be cleared at any time in your browser.

No analytics cookies are set until analytics is active (see Section 4). Google Analytics 4 does not store your raw IP address.

4. Your Consent & How to Change It

If you visit from the EU, EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, analytics stays switched off until you choose “Accept” on the cookie banner. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and the site works normally. Visitors from other regions have analytics enabled by default, as permitted by local law.

You can also clear this site's cookies and site data in your browser, which resets the banner.

5. Embedded Video

Episode trailers are embedded from YouTube using the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com mode. The player loads with the page rather than waiting for a click, so your browser contacts YouTube (Google) when the trailers come into view — but in this mode YouTube sets no cookies until you actually play a video. Once you play, YouTube may set its own cookies; that playback is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Where Data Goes

Website analytics is processed by Google (Google Analytics). Data may be transferred to and processed on Google's servers outside Japan and the EEA, including in the United States, subject to Google's applicable data-transfer safeguards. Google Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months.
