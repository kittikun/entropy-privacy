Privacy Policy

The Aurora Chronicles — by Entropy

Effective date: February 14, 2026 · Last updated: June 3, 2026

1. Who We Are

The Aurora Chronicles is developed by kittikun and published by Entropy, a sole proprietorship (個人事業主) based in Tokyo, Japan. This policy applies to The Aurora Chronicles on all platforms, including Steam, iOS, and Android.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the game experience:

  • Save Data: Your game progress is stored locally on your device. On Steam, save data is also synced via Steam Cloud. We do not currently store saves on our own servers.
  • Choice Statistics: When you make in-game choices, your selections are recorded to show community statistics (e.g., what percentage of players chose each option). Choices are linked to an anonymous identifier automatically generated by Firebase on your device. This data is stored on Supabase (cloud database). Other players only see aggregated percentages (e.g., “65% of players chose option A”), never individual choices.
  • Purchases (mobile only): On iOS and Android, in-app purchases are processed by Apple and Google. We use RevenueCat to manage purchase entitlements; RevenueCat receives an app-generated user identifier, your purchase and transaction history, and basic device information so we can unlock the content you bought and restore it across reinstalls. We never receive or store your payment card details.
  • Analytics & Crash Reports: We collect anonymous gameplay analytics to improve game quality, identify bugs, and understand how players engage with content. This data may include session duration, device information (OS, language, screen resolution), app version, and in-game events such as episode progress and menu interactions. On desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux), analytics and crash/performance diagnostics are processed by Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services). On mobile (iOS, Android), analytics are processed by Google (Google Analytics for Firebase) and crash reports — including device type, OS version, and crash logs — by Firebase Crashlytics. We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information through analytics, and we do not link analytics data to individual user accounts.
  • Push Notifications (mobile only): If you opt in to release notifications (for example, by tapping “Notify me when the next episode releases”), Firebase Cloud Messaging generates a device registration token so we can send you a notification when a new episode becomes available. Notifications are delivered as broadcasts grouped by language, and the token is used solely to deliver these messages — it is not used to identify you. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings or in the in-game Settings menu. Push notifications are not used on desktop (Steam) platforms.

The game does not require an account. Your save data stays local to your device (or synced via Steam Cloud on Steam). Choice statistics are recorded using an anonymous Firebase identifier, which cannot be used to identify you personally.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not store or use IP addresses or location data for tracking purposes.
  • Analytics across all platforms are limited to anonymous, aggregated data (see Section 2). We do not use analytics to identify or track individual players.
  • We do not serve advertisements.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share personal data for advertising purposes. We share data only with the service providers necessary to operate the game, as described in Section 4.

4. Third-Party Services

The following third-party services may process data on our behalf:

  • Firebase (Google): Provides anonymous authentication (Firebase Authentication) used to key your choice statistics, and delivers optional episode-release push notifications on mobile (Firebase Cloud Messaging). Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: Stores choice statistics data linked to your anonymous Firebase identifier. Subject to Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies): Collects anonymous gameplay analytics and diagnostics (crash and performance data) on desktop platforms (Windows, macOS, Linux). Subject to Unity's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase & Firebase Crashlytics: Collects anonymous usage data and crash data on mobile (iOS and Android) to help us improve the game. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Steam (Valve): Handles platform accounts, purchases, and Steam Cloud save sync. Subject to Steam's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple: Handles App Store purchases and accounts on iOS. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play: Handles purchases and accounts on Android. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: Manages in-app purchase entitlements on mobile (iOS and Android). Receives an app-generated user identifier, purchase and transaction history, and device information to unlock and restore purchased content. Subject to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

We do not have access to your payment information on any platform.

5. Analytics Data Retention

Anonymous analytics data is retained according to each provider's standard retention policies: Unity Gaming Services retains data for up to 12 months, and Google Analytics retains data for up to 14 months. These periods may be adjusted in their respective dashboards. We do not use analytics to identify individual players.

6. Data Storage & Security

Choice statistics are stored on Supabase. Anonymous authentication is handled by Firebase (Google Cloud). We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Some of our service providers (Firebase, Supabase) operate servers outside Japan, including in the United States. By using the game, you consent to your data being transferred to and processed in these jurisdictions, subject to those providers' applicable data transfer safeguards.

7. Children's Privacy

The Aurora Chronicles is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of the choice-statistics data associated with your anonymous identifier
  • Play without an account — the game requires none, and saves stay local (or on Steam Cloud)

To make a request, contact us using the details in Section 10 below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
